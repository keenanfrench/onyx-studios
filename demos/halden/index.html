FAQ

Good questions

Are the treatments safe for kids and pets?

We explain exactly what we’re using before we start and tell you how long to keep everyone off treated areas, which is usually just until surfaces are dry. Pets and kids can stay with you or at a neighbour’s while we work.

How often should I have a pest treatment?

For most homes, once a year keeps spiders, ants and cockroaches under control. For termites, the Australian Standard recommends a timber pest inspection at least every 12 months, or more often in high-risk areas.

Do I need to leave the house?

For most general treatments you can stay home and simply avoid the treated rooms for a few hours. We’ll tell you exactly what to expect when you book.

How do I prepare for a treatment?

Clear the floor around skirting boards, put away food and pet bowls, and give us access to the garden, sub-floor and roof space if they’re being treated. We’ll send you a short checklist before the visit.

Do you do end-of-lease treatments for rentals?

Yes. We can treat before you move out and provide the paperwork your real estate agent asks for.
