The brief

Carpet cleaning is bought on two questions: will it actually look better, and what will it really cost? The top local operators answer neither. Their sites have no prices, guarantees worded three different ways, and long quote forms before you see a number.

The approach

Navy and aqua for clean water and trust, with a bold grotesque headline. The hero is a draggable before and after, so the result is the first thing you see. Below it, prices are on the page: three packages and a build-your-own calculator, plus one clearly worded bond-back guarantee for renters.

Signature feature

An instant price calculator. Tap a home size or add rooms, rugs, stairs and lounge seats, and a fixed, GST-inclusive price updates live. “Lock in this price” carries the exact quote into the booking form.

What was built
  • Conversion-focused one-page site
  • Drag to compare before and after
  • Live price calculator
  • End of lease guarantee section
  • Reviews carousel & FAQ
  • Sticky call and quote bar on mobile
