The brief

A new photography and film studio needed to look established from day one and turn enquiries into booked shoots quickly, without any portfolio history to lean on.

The approach

The whole identity is built around colour temperature: Tungsten orange, Daylight blue and Bone, the Kelvin scale every cinematographer works with. A custom aperture-iris animation sets the tone, and the process section scrolls like a production schedule.
