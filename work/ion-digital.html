The brief

A brand-new ads agency needed to look credible to local service businesses (contractors, gyms, med spas) straight away, with one clear action: book a free ad audit.

The approach

A dark charcoal base with a single electric-blue accent reads as modern and performance-driven. With no case studies yet, the site sells the method instead: a three-step process and an honest “our approach” section in place of made-up testimonials.
